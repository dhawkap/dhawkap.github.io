Hi! I am Priya. I'm a second-year PhD student in the Human Centered Design and Engineering department at the University of Washington.

I'm broadly interested in thinking about **ethical, critical, and feminist data visualizations** alongside developing tools and strategies to support visualization designers in visually representing [protected demographic data](https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/small-business/3-who-protected-employment-discrimination) such as race, caste, gender, disability among others. I'm also curious about **open data practices and policies** and how these are misappropriated to support growing **data-driven misinformation** about marginalized groups around contentious political issues such as displacement and migration in the US.

In my Master's research, I explored [anthropographic approaches](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3544548.3581086) for visualizing racial demographic data to convey the diversity in lived experiences of underrepresented groups. I completed my MS in Computer Science from the University of Calgary in 2023 where I was part of the [Interactions Lab](https://ilab.ucalgary.ca/) and was advised by [Dr.Wesley Willett](http://www.wjwillett.net/) in the [Data Experience Lab](https://dataexperience.cpsc.ucalgary.ca). 

I have a BA in Computer Science from [Colgate University](https://www.colgate.edu/) where I also worked as a data analyst in equity and diversity. I am from the small island nation of [Mauritius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritius). Outside of research, you can find me reading or hanging out in the sun.
