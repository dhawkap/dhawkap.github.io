Hi! I am Priya. I’m a 4th year PhD candidate in the Human Centered Design and Engineering department at the University of Washington, advised by Dr. Jason Young and Dr. Jennifer Turns. 

I’m a data visualization researcher interested in the **rhetorical function of data and visualizations in the spread of online propaganda**. I study how open data and visualization best practices/conventions get repurposed to lend an air of objectivity to partisan narratives in social media discourse in the US.  I’m also interested in critical and feminist approaches to design **countervisualizations** that contest or reframe partisan narratives in response to emerging **propagandist visualizations**. 

In my Master's research, I explored [anthropographic approaches](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3544548.3581086) for visualizing racial demographic data to convey the diversity in lived experiences of underrepresented groups. I completed my MS in Computer Science from the University of Calgary in 2023 where I was part of the [Interactions Lab](https://ilab.ucalgary.ca/) and was advised by [Dr.Wesley Willett](http://www.wjwillett.net/) in the [Data Experience Lab](https://dataexperience.cpsc.ucalgary.ca). 

I have a BA in Computer Science from [Colgate University](https://www.colgate.edu/) where I also worked as a data visualization designer. I am from the small island nation of [Mauritius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritius). Outside of research, you can find me reading or hanging out in the sun.
