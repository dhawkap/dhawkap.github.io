Hi! I am Priya. I'm currently a PhD student in Human Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Washington. 

My research is at the intersection of information visualization, equity, protest and accountability. I am broadly interested in equitable ways of humanizing demographic data, specifically data about race. My Master's research explores the use of demographically-diverse anthropographics for visualizing racial data to convey the humanity and lived experiences of the people behind the data. 

I completed my MS in Computer Science from the University of Calgary in 2023 where I was part of the [Interactions Lab](https://ilab.ucalgary.ca/) and was advised by [Dr.Wesley Willett](http://www.wjwillett.net/) in the (Data Experience Lab)[https://dataexperience.cpsc.ucalgary.ca]. 

I have a BA in Computer Science from [Colgate University](https://www.colgate.edu/) where I also worked as a data analyst in equity and diversity. I am from the small island nation of [Mauritius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritius). Outside of research, you can find me reading or hanging out in the sun.
